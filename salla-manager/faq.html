Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app need my Salla password?

No. Installation uses Salla OAuth.

Can it manage more than one store?

Yes. Each authorized store receives its own connection and store ID.

What can it manage?

Depending on approved scopes: products, orders, customers, categories, brands, branches, marketing data, reviews, taxes, metadata and store settings.

How do I revoke access?

Uninstall the app from the Salla store.
